# Product Steering

## Source Of Truth
- Use this document as the product-level guide for the portfolio site.
- Treat repository content and page copy as the authoritative expression of scope when this document is silent.
- Keep this document aligned with the current site behavior; do not turn it into a wishlist.

## Product Purpose
- Present Laura Sirvent's portfolio as an editorial, highly art-directed website rather than a generic gallery.
- Use the site to showcase commissioned work, personal experimentation, and a distinct author voice across motion, design, and storytelling.
- Preserve the playful, tactile, and character-driven tone visible in page copy, stickers, motion, and interactive details.

## Core User Journeys
- Let visitors land on a strong personal introduction on the home page.
- Let visitors browse selected professional projects through the `Work` index and individual project pages.
- Let visitors explore ongoing experiments through the `Sketchbook` page.
- Let visitors access biography, representation, and contact information through the `About` page and footer/header links.

## Content Domains
- Use `works` entries for polished portfolio case studies with media-first presentation and narrative detail.
- Use `playground` entries for lighter-weight experiments, notes, images, videos, text fragments, and 3D sketches.
- Use stickers, typography, sound, and theme shifts as part of the product experience, not as purely decorative afterthoughts.

## Experience Principles
- Favor expressive presentation over dense navigation.
- Keep media central: thumbnails, embedded video, images, and 3D previews should carry the experience.
- Preserve direct authorial voice in written content; avoid flattening copy into corporate portfolio language.
- Keep interaction playful but readable on touch and desktop.
- Maintain contact and representation paths as easy-to-find, production-safe affordances.

## Page Expectations
- Home: introduce Laura and establish the site tone immediately.
- Work index: provide a scan-friendly overview of portfolio pieces.
- Work detail: foreground hero media, project context, and supporting stills or credits.
- Sketchbook: group entries by date and support mixed media.
- About: combine biography, portrait, representation, and contact details.

## Change Boundaries
- Add new pages or sections only when they support the existing portfolio, sketchbook, or contact flows.
- Prefer extending existing content models before introducing new ones.
- Avoid product changes that make the site feel like a standard template or remove its playful identity.
- Avoid backend or account-based features unless there is explicit evidence they are needed.
